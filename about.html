Built on reliable supply

Macdon Dynamic Multi Link has spent years making sure Nigerian building sites never stall for materials.

Started to solve a supply problem

Macdon Dynamic Multi Link was founded to close a gap contractors know well: ordering materials is easy, but getting them to site on time rarely is.

We built the business around that one problem — stocking the core materials sites reorder most, and running our own fleet so delivery is never the bottleneck.

Today we supply iron rods, cement, nails, binding wire and more to contractors and developers across Lagos and beyond.

What guides how we operate

Reliability

If we confirm an order, it ships. Sites plan their schedules around our delivery dates.

Quality

We source from certified mills and manufacturers, not open-market resale, on every material we stock.

Fair Pricing

Transparent rates for retail orders, and real trade pricing for contractors buying in bulk.

How we've grown

Year 1

Founded in Lagos

Started supplying iron rods and cement to local contractors from a single yard.

Growth

Added our own fleet

Brought delivery in-house to guarantee timelines instead of relying on third-party transport.

Today

Serving sites nationwide

Supplying contractors and developers across multiple states, with same-day delivery in Lagos.

The fleet is the promise
